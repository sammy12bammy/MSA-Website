About Us

MSA Services provides technical development teams to build digital products for all market types. With decades of experience in designing and building scalable, secure and cloud-based solutions, MSA Services will guide you through every step of the product development lifecycle. CTO Julian Roux-Stevens leads the team with more than 30 years experience in web development, AI and big data across many B2C and B2B markets in the US, Europe and Australasia.

Specialising in a design-led and rapid prototyping approach to development, MSA Services aims to deliver results fast - on time and at cost. With a broad range of skills covering all the major cloud platform providers, plus front-end technologies to cover multi-device solutions, MSA Services can build to your requirements. MSA Services has a scalable team based in Europe and the US, providing a cost effective, multi-timezone solution for when time and cost are key pressure points.
