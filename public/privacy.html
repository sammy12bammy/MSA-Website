Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2025

MSA Advisors (“MSA Services,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information when you interact with our website and services. 1. Information We Collect The only personal information we collect through our website is information you voluntarily provide to us via the Contact Us form. This may include: Name Email address Phone number (if provided) Company name (if provided) Any other information you choose to share in your message We do not collect personal information automatically when you visit our website, other than standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) necessary for security and basic functionality. 2. Cookies and Tracking We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or advertising technologies on our website. The only data collected is what you submit voluntarily through the Contact Us form. 3. How We Use Your Information We use the information you provide solely to: Respond to your inquiries Communicate with you regarding our services Maintain basic records of communications We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. 4. Data Sharing We may share your information only in the following cases: With trusted service providers who help us operate our email or website systems (under strict confidentiality obligations). Where required by law, regulation, or legal process. In connection with a merger, acquisition, or restructuring of MSA Advisors. 5. Data Security We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. 6. Data Retention We retain your contact information only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain reasonable business records, unless a longer retention period is required by law. 7. Your Rights Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to: Request access to the personal information we hold about you. Request correction or deletion of your information. Restrict or object to our processing of your data. Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@msa-advisors.com. 8. International Transfers If you are located outside the United States, please note that your information may be transferred and processed in the United States, where our systems are located. 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with the date of revision. 10. Contact Us If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us: MSA Advisors – Privacy Office Email: info@msa-advisors.com
